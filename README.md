**Lightening**: A privacy-first web browser for Windows: Screenshot protection, a PIN lock, built-in ad blocking, and no tracking.

**Why Lightening?**
Your browser shows your most private things: client dashboards, admin panels, bank accounts, private chats. Yet anything on your screen can be captured by a screenshot or a screen recorder without you noticing, and most browsers come with trackers, ads and accounts you never asked for.

Lightening keeps what's on your screen private, keeps your data on your own computer, and stays out of your way.

**1.0 Privacy and security**
-	Screenshot protection: the browser window is hidden from screenshots, screen recorders and screen-sharing apps, and the PrintScreen key is blocked while Lightening is in focus.
-	App lock: protect the browser with a 4 - 8-digit PIN. It can lock automatically after a period of inactivity and when Windows locks or goes to sleep.
-	Ad and tracker blocking: built in and powered by the EasyList and EasyPrivacy filter lists, which update weekly. Allow a site with one click.
-	Private tabs: nothing is kept. Cookies and site data are wiped when the last private tab closes.
-	Privacy mode: blur every image on every page with one click (Ctrl+Shift+P). Choose light, medium or strong blur.
-	Site permissions: websites must ask before using your camera, microphone, location or notifications. Your answer is remembered and can be changed at any time.
-	Clear browsing data: clear it whenever you like, or automatically every time you close the browser.

**2.0 Tabs and windows**
-	Tab groups with names and colors that you can collapse.
-	Vertical tabs in a sidebar, or the classic tab strip on top.
-	Pinned and muted tabs, plus tab search across every window (Ctrl+Shift+A).
-	Multiple windows: drag a tab out to open it in a new one.
-	Restores your tabs, groups and windows when you start the browser again.

**3.0 Everyday browsing**
-	Bookmarks with folders, a bookmarks bar and a full bookmark manager.
-	Reading mode for clean, distraction-free articles (F9).
-	Downloads page, history with search, and find in page.
-	Back/forward history menus, a home button and per-site zoom.
-	Light, dark or system theme, a compact toolbar, and a choice of which toolbar buttons to show.
-	Search with Google, DuckDuckGo, Bing or Brave Search.

**Requirements**
-	Windows 10 or Windows 11 (64-bit or 32-bit)
-	About 400 MB of free disk space

**Installing**
-	Download the setup file and open it.
-	If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC": click More info -> Run anyway. Windows shows this for apps from independent developers that aren't code-signed by a large publisher. It doesn't mean the app is harmful (see [Is it safe?](#is-it-safe) below).
-	Choose whether to install for just you or all users of the computer.
-	Choose where to install it, or keep the suggested folder.
-	Click Install, then Finish to open Lightening.

**Is it safe?**
Yes. Here is exactly what Lightening does and doesn't do:
-	No account, no sign-in. You never have to give Lightening your email or any personal details.
-	No tracking or analytics. Lightening doesn't collect usage data and doesn't report what you browse to anyone.
-	Your data stays on your computer. History, bookmarks, settings and downloads are stored locally in `%APPDATA%\Lightening`, and nothing is synced to a server.
-	It only connects where you go, plus once a week to download the public ad-blocking filter lists (EasyList and EasyPrivacy).
-	Built on Chromium, the same engine as Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge, with every website running in its own security sandbox.
-	Websites can't access your camera, microphone or location without your permission, and can't take screen captures of the browser.

**A note on screenshot protection:** Lightening hides its window from screenshot, screen-recording and screen-sharing software. No software can stop someone from photographing the screen with a phone camera.
